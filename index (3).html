About the Programme

Why do we need online banking awareness?

What is Online Banking?

Online banking allows people to perform banking activities using a mobile phone or computer. We can check our balance, transfer money, pay bills and use UPI without visiting a bank branch.


Because banking is now digital, users should know how to identify suspicious messages, fake websites and fraudulent payment requests.

Our Main Goal

To teach basic safety habits that can help students and other users use digital banking more carefully.

Step-by-Step Banking Safety

Follow these simple steps whenever you use online banking.

1

Use a Strong Password

Use a unique and difficult-to-guess password for banking accounts.

  • Do not use your name or birthday.
  • Do not reuse your banking password.
2

Never Share OTP

OTP is private information used to verify certain transactions or logins.

  • Never tell your OTP to callers.
  • Do not send OTP screenshots.
3

Protect Your UPI PIN

Keep your UPI PIN secret and enter it only when you intentionally authorise a payment.

  • Check the recipient first.
  • Never enter a PIN just to receive money.
4

Check Links

Fake links may look similar to real bank websites or services.

  • Do not click unknown links.
  • Open the bank app or website yourself.
5

Secure Your Phone

Your phone may contain banking apps and sensitive information.

  • Use screen lock.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Avoid unknown apps.
6

Check Transactions

Regularly check account statements and transaction alerts.

  • Report unknown transactions quickly.
  • Keep transaction reference details.
7

Avoid Screen Sharing

Fraudsters may ask you to install remote-access applications.

  • Do not give strangers remote access.
  • Contact your bank directly.
8

Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive banking activity on unknown or unsecured networks.

  • Prefer trusted networks.
  • Use mobile data when appropriate.
9

Use Official Contact Details

If you have a problem, contact the bank through its official app, website or card information.

  • Do not trust numbers sent by strangers.

Common Online Banking Frauds

Knowing common fraud methods helps us recognise warning signs.

📩 Phishing

Fake emails or messages may ask you to click a link and enter banking information.

Warning sign: Urgent message asking you to “verify” your account.

📞 Fake Bank Calls

Someone may pretend to be a bank employee and ask for OTP, PIN or other confidential information.

Warning sign: Caller asks for secret information.

💸 Fake UPI Requests

A fraudster may send a payment request and try to convince you to approve it.

Warning sign: You are asked to approve an unexpected payment.

📱 Remote Access Scam

Someone may ask you to install an app so they can “help” with a banking problem.

Warning sign: Stranger asks to control or view your screen.

What To Do If You Are Scammed?

  1. Contact your bank immediately using an official channel.
  2. Secure or block the affected card/account if advised by the bank.
  3. Save transaction details, screenshots and reference numbers.
  4. Report the incident to the appropriate cybercrime authority.
  5. Do not send more money to the person claiming they can recover your money.

For India: suspected financial cyber fraud can be reported through the official government cybercrime reporting system and helpline 1930. Always verify the current details through official government sources.
